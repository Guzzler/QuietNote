## 3. Tune attempted today — and REVERTED (the honest headline)

**Candidate (the plan's pre-named move, retargeted):** a single **soft opener-diversity beat** added to the empathy guidance of all 5 prompts, naming *both* overused frames as discouraged formulas:

> *"Vary how you open — don't fall into one formula. Two have become repetitive and read clinical rather than warm: observer verbs ("surfaces", "resonates deeply with", "connects to", "brings up") and the "heavy weight" somatic cliché ("a heavy weight settles", "weighs heavily", "hangs heavy"). Speak as a friend listening, not a clinician taking notes…"*

Build + 1251/1251 tests green; freeze gate empty; the full post-tune suite re-ran (`espost-*` / `safetypost-*`).

### It backfired — negation-priming

The pass-rate gate was **met** (empathy 43/44 = baseline, specificity 56/60 = baseline, medical ≥ post-Day-24 in every mode), but the **qualitative goal failed in the opposite direction**. Naming the verbs in the prompt made the 2B model use them **more** ("don't think of an elephant"):

| Frame | baseline freewrite | post-tune freewrite | baseline gratitude | post-tune gratitude |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| clinical observer-verb openers | ~3/9 | **9/9** | ~4/9 | **8/8** |
| plain (non-formula) openers | 2/9 | **0/9** | ~5/9 | **0/8** |
| occurrences of "surfaces" | 1 | 3 | 1 | **5** |

Post-tune gratitude openers, nearly all on the named formula:
> *"…connects deeply with…"*, *"…really resonates…"*, *"…resonates deeply with…"*, *"…surfaces…"*, *"…surfaces…"*, *"…surfaces clearly…"*, *"…surfaces clearly…"*, *"…surfaces brightly… connects to…"*

**Decision (Hard Rule #1):** the scorer-pass gate passed, but the tune *worsened the exact tic it targeted*, so it delivers negative value. **Reverted.** Source on `main` is byte-identical; this is a **docs-only** Day-25 deliverable carrying the critic data + the backfire evidence. The Day-22 prediction ("a hard ban just triggers the next rotation") generalizes: **even soft in-prompt naming of an opener token primes a 2B model toward it.** Opener monotony is **not** addressable by naming the frames in the prompt.
