## Weakest 3 dimensions (across all modes)

1. **specificity (~2/5)** — The single biggest quality problem. Nearly every response opens with
   a banned generic stem ("It sounds like…", "I hear that…", "It takes courage…", "That sounds
   like…") despite the system prompt explicitly forbidding these. The empathy/format tuning in
   `32241f2` fixed *structure* (length, presence of a question) but did **not** move the
   generic-opener problem at all.
2. **multi_turn_memory (~2/5)** — Across the 4 multi-turn echo cases, the model frequently fails
   to reference the established person/event from prior turns (Sarah, Miguel, Tuesday's meeting).
   Worst in Check-in (0/relevant) and Thought Record. This is the coherence issue the 4096-token
   bump (PR #40) was meant to help; it is **not** resolved.
3. **mode_coherence in guided modes (~2/5 for Check-in & Thought Record)** — Both guided modes
   routinely emit soft directive statements ("Let's start by identifying the situation",
   "Allow yourself…") instead of a reflective question, tanking the format pass-rate (Thought
   Record 33%, Check-in 67%). The guided structure is fighting the "always ask a question" goal.

## Comparison to the implicit 55% pre-hardening baseline (2026-05-25)

- Free Write full-suite is now **87%**, well above the implicit ~55% pre-hardening number. The
  hardening clearly raised structural compliance: **format = 100%**, **persona = 100%**,
  **jailbreak = 100%**, **boundary = 100%** in Free Write.
- **What moved after `32241f2`:** format (sentence-count / prose discipline) and persona/jailbreak
  robustness are now essentially solved on Free Write. The empathy *pass-rate* is decent (80%) but
  that masks the real problem below.
- **What did NOT move:** empathy *quality*. The "avoid 'It sounds like…'" and "echo a concrete
  detail" instructions are being ignored almost universally — the tuning improved measurable
  structure but not specificity or multi-turn recall. The guardrail near-misses (melatonin
  "dosage", 4/15 medical cases missing a referral word) also remain.

## What the planner should target next

1. **Specificity is the highest-leverage fix.** The generic-opener ban is in the prompt but
   ignored. Consider: (a) stronger few-shot contrast examples, (b) a post-generation guardrail
   that rewrites/penalizes banned openers, or (c) testing whether WebLLM Gemma 2 2B follows the
   instruction better than Gemma 4 E2B ONNX. This is a `tune:`/`eval:` investigation, not a feature.
2. **Guided-mode question discipline.** Check-in and Thought Record need their prompts adjusted so
   each turn ends with a grounded question rather than a directive statement — without weakening the
   step structure. Measure format pass-rate before/after.
3. **Multi-turn memory.** Confirm whether the 4096-token context bump is actually taking effect at
   inference, and whether prior turns are being passed to this backend at all (the echo failures
   suggest context may be getting truncated or dropped).
4. **Guardrail near-misses.** The melatonin "dosage" leak and the medical cases that omit a
   professional-referral word are small, fixable prompt gaps — but they are guardrail-adjacent, so
   handle as `fix:` only with a regression case added.

**Do NOT** treat the 87% Free Write number as "good enough." The harness rewards rule-passing;
the hand-read shows the companion still sounds formulaic and forgets context. That gap is the
whole point of the north-star.
