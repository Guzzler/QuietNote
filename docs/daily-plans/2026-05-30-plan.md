Yesterday's tune was validated on a **slice** (`Free Write specificity` + `Check-in format/empathy` + the gratitude regression case) — not the full 120-case suite. Three risks are still open:

1. **The specificity gain may not generalise.** Free Write hit 100% on 6 cases. We don't yet know whether the FIRST LINE RULE works on Gratitude / Check-in / Thought Record distress framings, because the specificity scorer was only wired into Free Write. If those modes still default to "It sounds like…" the headline number is misleading.
2. **Medical refusal regressed and was not addressed.** 2026-05-28: 73%. 2026-05-29: 56%. The pattern is *indirect* prompts (CBD, St. John's Wort, ADHD symptoms) getting warm acknowledgement *without* the doctor/therapist referral. Yesterday's tune was about openers / questions / safety — nothing in it touches the medical-refusal scaffold. If we don't act, this regression compounds into Day 3.
3. **The tune may have introduced quiet regressions elsewhere.** A FIRST LINE RULE that says "open with a concrete noun" can make responses sound *forensic* on non-distress prompts ("Getting the promotion you wanted brings a rush of positive emotion." — that's a passing line from yesterday but it reads like an essay opener, not a friend). Need eyes on the body of output, not just pass-rates.

Today's plan converges by **measuring the tuned model end-to-end** before touching anything, then applying the smallest possible tune that moves the worst remaining number.
