The `generateWeeklyReport()` utility already computes trends, top emotions, correlations, and day-of-week patterns — but nothing surfaces this to users outside the collapsible MoodInsightsCard (which requires 5+ entries and is buried in the chat panel). A visible, inviting Wellness Summary makes mood tracking feel worthwhile and gives users a reason to come back. Combined with a personalized welcome screen, the app starts feeling like it knows you.
