### Next Mode: CBT Thought Record (BUILD TODAY)

**Why CBT next:**
- Cognitive Behavioral Therapy thought records are the most evidence-based structured journaling technique for mental health
- They help users identify and reframe negative thinking patterns — the core value proposition of a reflective journaling app
- A 2B-class model can effectively facilitate the structured steps (situation description, thought identification, emotion naming, evidence checking, balanced thought) without needing deep reasoning
- This differentiates QuietNote from simple gratitude/check-in apps and positions it as a genuine mental wellness tool
- The 5-step structure is more ambitious than the 3-step gratitude/check-in modes, proving the guided mode framework scales

**How it works:**
- User selects "Thought Record" mode from the mode selector
- 5-step guided flow:
  1. **Situation:** "What happened? Describe the situation briefly."
  2. **Automatic Thought:** "What went through your mind? What were you thinking?"
  3. **Emotion:** "What emotions did you feel? How intense were they (1-10)?"
  4. **Evidence:** "What evidence supports or contradicts this thought?"
  5. **Balanced Thought:** "What's a more balanced way to think about this?"
- After completing all 5 steps, the AI provides a brief CBT-style summary that highlights the reframe
- Same safety guardrails (medical refusal, crisis detection) apply

**Model integration:**
- New system prompt for CBT mode that guides users through cognitive restructuring
- The AI should NOT act as a therapist but facilitate the structured self-reflection
- Key instruction: "Help the user identify the thought pattern and gently guide them to consider alternative perspectives. You are a journaling facilitator, not a therapist."

**UI/UX:**
- Fourth pill in JournalingModeSelector: "Thought Record" with `Brain` icon (already imported in App.tsx)
- `ThoughtRecordGuide` component with 5-step progress indicator
- Step indicator adapts from 3 to 5 segments
- Welcome card shows CBT-appropriate intro text
