## A. Today's objective

Make the writing and reading surface look and feel like a paper journal — serif type on the writing/reading surface, one accent color on the writing path, flattened card-in-card chrome, no hover-glow on entries — Fundamental Problem 4 ("feels like a tech demo, not a journal"), roadmap item A2, plus the small A1 leftovers (icon-tile softening, footer `â€¢` mojibake).

## B. Why this matters

A1 fixed *what* the empty state says; A2 fixes *how the whole app reads*. Today the conversation renders as SMS bubbles (solid indigo-600 user bubble, `hover:shadow-md` glow, 14px system sans) inside a white card inside another white card — the visual language of a chat SaaS demo, not a quiet page someone opens at 11pm. The roadmap calls A2 " 'tech demo → journal' made concrete and … screenshot-verifiable." Serif at a comfortable reading size, a single calm accent, and paper-flat surfaces are the cheapest possible way to change what the app *feels* like without touching a single behavior.
