Connect mood tracking to the journaling experience by adding mood-aware prompt suggestions after mood logging, and introduce a weekly reflection entry point that uses accumulated mood data to guide deeper self-reflection (Objectives 1 + 2).

## B. Why This Matters

Right now mood tracking and journaling are parallel activities — users log moods and write journal entries, but the two don't inform each other. When a user logs "anxious," the app should help them explore that feeling through journaling. And after a week of mood data, the app should be able to say "here's what your week looked like — want to reflect on it?" This is the bridge between tracking emotions and actually processing them.
