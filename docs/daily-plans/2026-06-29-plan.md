## B. Why this matters

- **It's the confirmed weakest tunable dimension.** The Day-25 full read
  ([`docs/critic-reports/2026-06-28.md`](../critic-reports/2026-06-28.md) §2.2)
  found empathy 43/44 and specificity 56/60 are healthy, but the *qualitative*
  weakness is real and pervasive: a **clear majority** of free-write/gratitude
  openers now route through one of two monotone formulas — the **"heavy weight"
  somatic cliché** ("a heavy weight settles", "weighs heavily", "hangs heavy") and
  the **clinical observer-verb frame** ("surfaces", "resonates deeply with",
  "connects to", "brings up"). It even bleeds into checkin and medical openers. The
  companion sounds like it has three sentences it rotates, not like a friend who
  listens differently each time. That is the live warmth debt.
- **We already know the obvious fix is wrong — and that's the whole point of doing
  it right.** Day-25 *attempted* the intuitive tune (name the overused verbs in the
  prompt and say "don't use them"). It **backfired hard via negation-priming**:
  naming the verbs drove clinical-verb openers from ~3/9 → **9/9** in free-write and
  ~4/9 → **8/8** in gratitude ("don't think of an elephant"). It passed the scorer
  gate but *worsened the exact tic*, and was reverted. The report's explicit
  conclusion: **opener monotony is not addressable by naming the frames in the
  prompt.** Today executes the report's *recommended* alternative — option (a),
  show good variety, never name the bad — which has never been tried.
- **Real users.** Every entry's first line is the moment the user decides whether
  they feel heard or processed. Breaking the formula so the opener reads fresh and
  human is the single highest-leverage warmth win on the board.

## E. Concrete implementation plan

### E.1 — Priority 1: the opener-exemplar rotation (the single source change)

**File:** `src/prompts/systemPrompts.ts` (`tune:` only — the one source file).

**The edit shape (positive-only — this is the entire discipline of today's tune):**
In each of the 5 prompts' FIRST LINE RULE, **replace the single-shape example set**
with a set that demonstrates **several distinct opening structures**, and add one
short positive variety nudge. Name good structures; **never** name "surfaces",
"resonates", "heavy weight", "weighs heavily", "hangs heavy", "connects to", "brings
up", or any other discouraged token. (Day-25 proved naming them primes them.)

**Suggested free-write replacement for `systemPrompts.ts:19`** (executor may refine
wording, but must preserve the *structure-variety* property):

> *Open by naming something concrete the user wrote — a person, a place, an event, a
> feeling word — and vary the shape of that opening line from reply to reply so it
> never reads like a formula. Different shapes that all work:*
> *• a plain statement of what happened — "You lost the promotion you'd worked two
> years for."*
> *• their own words echoed back — "'I cried in the bathroom' — those words carry a
> lot."*
> *• leading straight into the specific question — "Anna's wedding fell apart on the
> day itself — what part still sits with you?"*
> *Speak the detail plainly and react to it as a friend would. If you cannot find a
> concrete detail to name, restate one of their own phrases back to them.*

Apply the same positive-variety treatment to the gratitude / checkin / thoughtrecord
/ (and any 5th) prompts' FIRST LINE RULE blocks, adapting the example wording to each
mode's voice but **keeping the three distinct shapes** (plain statement / echoed
words / question-led). For guided modes, keep the existing mode rules intact (e.g.
thoughtrecord's ACKNOWLEDGE-BEFORE-STEP RULE at `:163` — the varied opener still
precedes the step name; the question-led shape must not collide with
"always end with a question").

**Hard constraints:**
- **Do NOT** add, re-add, or reference any negative opener-verb list anywhere
  (prompt OR scorer). The verbs go nowhere near the text.
- **Do NOT** touch: the FIRST LINE RULE *stock-phrase ban* (the existing "It sounds
  like / I hear that / …" list stays — it's positive-framed-by-exclusion of generic
  filler and has held since Day-1 with zero stock-stem fires), the MEDICAL RULE
  (incl. GENERAL-TERMS REFERRAL), the UNINTELLIGIBLE INPUT RULE, the SAFETY CARVEOUT,
  the crisis-path question requirement, the AI disclaimer.
- **Do NOT** touch: `crisisDetection.ts`, `responseGuardrails.ts`,
  `sessionContext.ts`, `evalRunner.ts`, `evalScorer.ts`, `EVAL_CASES`.
