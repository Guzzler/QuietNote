## B. Why this matters

- **Real users:** when someone writes "I've been taking 10mg of melatonin and still can't sleep," a companion that parrots *"taking ten milligrams of melatonin nightly…"* back reads like it's engaging with the clinical specifics before deferring. A companion that says *"Sleep that won't come even after what you've tried is exhausting — a doctor can help you look at this"* is warmer, safer, and doesn't appear to validate the dose. The fix is good behavior on its own merits, not just a scorer-pleasing trick.
- **Measurement honesty:** the Day-22 critic ([`docs/critic-reports/2026-06-24.md`](../critic-reports/2026-06-24.md) §2.4 / §4 move #2) showed ~half of all medical/jailbreak "failures" are the model **correctly referring while echoing the user's own banned word**. Until this is resolved, the medical pass-rates *understate* real safety, and the daily ledger reads worse than the app behaves.
- **This is the next-highest unstarted item.** The roadmap (Tracks A–D) is fully drained; the daily eval→tune loop owns what remains. Day 23 (PR #69, MEDICAL PRECEDENCE) closed the **genuine referral-omission gap** — thoughtrecord medical jumped 31% → 75% in the 06-25 re-run. Every one of the 4 residual failures is now the *echo* pattern, not omission. This plan finishes that thread.

### E.1 — Priority 1: the prompt beat (the only source change)

**File:** `src/prompts/systemPrompts.ts`

**Change:** append ONE sentence to the **MEDICAL / HEALTH / MEDICATION RULE** block. That block is duplicated verbatim in all 5 prompt constants — edit **all five** so the modes stay consistent:
- `SYSTEM_INSTRUCTION` (free-write, line ~14)
- `GRATITUDE_SYSTEM_INSTRUCTION` (line ~68)
- `CHECKIN_MORNING_INSTRUCTION` (line ~103)
- `CHECKIN_EVENING_INSTRUCTION` (line ~129)
- `THOUGHT_RECORD_INSTRUCTION` (line ~157)

**Exact text to append** (after *"…Acknowledgement first; referral always; no clinical content."*):

> *"GENERAL-TERMS REFERRAL: when you refer them on a health topic, name the concern only in general terms (e.g. "what you're taking", "that medication", "how you've been sleeping") — do NOT repeat the specific medication, dose, milligram amount, supplement, or remedy name they used. Ground your opening empathy in the feeling or situation (the exhaustion, the worry, the sleeplessness), not in the clinical term."*

**Why this exact shape:**
- "Ground your opening empathy in the feeling or situation, not the clinical term" reconciles it with the **FIRST LINE RULE** so the model still opens warmly (empathy 44/44 preserved) — it just redirects *what* it echoes on medical turns.
- It is **scoped inside the MEDICAL RULE**, which only governs health-topic turns, so it cannot bleed into ordinary-turn empathy echo.
- It explicitly lists "milligram amount" and "dose" to nail `medical-2.7-regression`.

**Also update the THOUGHT_RECORD `MEDICAL PRECEDENCE` clause** (line ~165): after *"…do NOT advise on dosage, timing, brand, or mechanism, and do NOT diagnose,"* append a short pointer so the two clauses don't appear to conflict:
> *"…and refer in general terms per the GENERAL-TERMS REFERRAL rule above (do not repeat the specific medication, dose, or supplement name)."*

**Do NOT touch:** the FIRST LINE RULE, UNINTELLIGIBLE INPUT RULE, SAFETY CARVEOUT, crisis-path question requirement, the disclaimer, `crisisDetection.ts`, `responseGuardrails.ts`, `sessionContext.ts`, `evalRunner.ts`, `evalScorer.ts`. The referral requirement itself ("MUST include doctor/therapist/…") is **unchanged** — we are *adding* a constraint, never weakening one.
